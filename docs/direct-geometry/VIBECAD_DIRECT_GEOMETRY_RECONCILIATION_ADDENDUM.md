## 1. Bounded VibeCAD exception

VC-DG-0 through VC-DG-6 are an explicit, bounded VibeCAD exception for the
structural and thermal direct-geometry core described by the source paper.
They do not reactivate generalized Structures, generalized Physics,
multiphysics, remote execution, historical high-fidelity Aero Steps 12-20, or
other advanced engineering expansion outside the current VibeCAD boundary.

VC-DG-7 is assigned to VibeCAD. Its optional status does not make its owner
undecided, and VibeMechanica has no implementation obligation. The work is not
started and does not block completion of VC-DG-0 through VC-DG-6. VibeCAD may
begin it only after VC-DG-6 is finished and the VibeCAD owner explicitly says to
begin. It earns no completion credit for VC-DG-0 through VC-DG-6 and no
completion credit for historical Aero Step 12 or any later Aero milestone.

The rationale is scope and sequencing, not uncertainty. VC-DG-0 through VC-DG-6
already define and qualify the required structural/thermal product. VC-DG-7 adds
a separate fluid method, conservation checks, benchmark set, and qualification
burden that are not prerequisites for that product and must not delay it or
duplicate VibeMechanica's advanced Aero program. Optional changes the VibeCAD
release gate, not ownership: VibeCAD still owns and tracks VC-DG-7.

## 2. VC-DG-0 consumes the reusable tester; it does not own it

The source paper's VC-DG-0 work package is interpreted as the **direct-geometry
acceptance profile** for an independently owned, reusable visible development
tester. VC-DG MUST NOT fork, duplicate, or become the lifecycle owner of the
launcher, authenticated loopback control channel, semantic UI discovery,
software cursor overlay, screenshot service, file-lifecycle routes, or generic
evidence receipt format.

The reusable tester's generic layer:

- launches the exact checkout and binds evidence to its exact process and
  executable;
- discovers enabled semantic menus and ribbon tabs from the live application;
- activates an exact semantic target in-process;
- draws only a plain cyan software cursor;
- never moves, clicks, confines, blocks, or injects input into the user's
  physical pointer or keyboard;
- exercises native create/open/save-as/save/close/reopen behavior with
  overwrite and unsaved-close refusal;
- captures visible screenshots and collision-resistant, fail-closed receipts;
- applies bounded readiness, request, and verification timeouts.

The VibeCAD repository profile may require or report domain surfaces such as
File, Tools, Macro, Aero, Model, McMaster, and later Analyze/direct-geometry
pages. Those names are configuration or profile expectations, not hard-coded
requirements of the reusable generic core. A profile must distinguish an
absent optional surface from a failed generic tester invariant.

Generic tester success closes only the reusable infrastructure prerequisite.
It does **not** prove selector correctness, representation integrity, physics,
solver integration, numerical convergence, qualification, or any other
VC-DG-1 through VC-DG-6 requirement.

## 3. Generic CfdOF is not governed Aero Step 12

The source paper's references to existing OpenFOAM/CfdOF capability mean that
the generic FreeCAD CfdOF module may remain available as a compatibility,
reference, fallback, or comparison surface. Presence, packaging, importability,
or repair of that generic module does not establish the governed VibeCADAero
Step 12 client, case identity, shared Analysis Runtime lifecycle, publication
authority, benchmark evidence, or qualification envelope.

Therefore:

- generic CfdOF compatibility earns zero Aero Step 12 completion credit;
- a conforming OpenFOAM/CfdOF comparison earns no direct-geometry method credit;
- VC-DG-7, if begun after VC-DG-6 is finished and the VibeCAD owner explicitly
  says to begin, requires its own embedded-flow method and independent
  comparison evidence;
- Aero Steps 12-20 remain historical/non-normative in VibeCAD; their future
  implementation belongs to VibeMechanica unless both repositories explicitly
  approve a later ownership change.

## 4. McMasterInsert remains separate retained work

The current McMasterInsert browse/import/cache/component-metadata/placement
surface remains a retained VibeCAD capability and must finish any already
started packaging, runtime, and acceptance work. It is neither the bundled
standard-fastener catalog nor part of VC-DG. Procurement intelligence, supplier
comparison, automated sourcing, generalized catalog expansion, and purchasing
authority are outside the active VibeCAD boundary.

## 5. Reusable tester separation invariant

The reusable tester remains a generic, independently versioned tool surface. Its
core may contain the launcher, loopback authentication, semantic discovery and
activation, plain cyan software cursor, file lifecycle, screenshots, evidence
receipts, timeouts, tests, and generic operator documentation. Domain-specific
roadmap rules, VC-DG algorithms, physics implementations, and product-only
requirements belong in consumer profiles or their owning modules, not in that
generic core.

## 6. Reconciled closure rule

The direct-geometry program is not complete until every applicable item in the
source paper's definition of done is satisfied under this addendum, VC-DG-0
through VC-DG-6 are closed with direct visible and automated evidence, and no
unresolved issue remains inside the declared bounded core. VC-DG-7 remains
outside that required closure unless separately authorized.
